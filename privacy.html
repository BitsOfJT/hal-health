Hal Health — Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026.

What this app is

Hal Health is a personal application operated by Jordan Thompson for his own use. It has exactly one user: its owner.

Data accessed

Through the Google Health API, using read-only permissions the owner grants, the app accesses the owner's own:

  • Activity and fitness data — steps, distance, exercise sessions, calories
  • Health metrics and measurements — heart rate, heart rate variability, weight, body fat
  • Sleep data — sleep sessions and stages

The app never writes to, modifies, or deletes data in the owner's Google account.

How the data is used

Only for the owner's personal fitness tracking: a personal training dashboard, nightly summaries, and progress tracking toward personal health goals.

Sharing

Data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or analytics.

Storage and retention

Data is processed on the owner's own systems. OAuth tokens are stored with restricted file permissions. Derived summaries (such as daily step totals) are kept as part of the owner's personal records.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To request deletion of stored data, contact the owner at the address below.

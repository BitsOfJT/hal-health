Hal Health

Hal Health is a private, single-user application operated by its owner, Jordan Thompson, for his own personal fitness tracking.

It reads the owner's own Google Health data — activity and fitness, health metrics, and sleep, all read-only — and uses it for a personal training dashboard and nightly activity summaries. It is not a public product: there are no sign-ups, no other users, no advertising, and no data is sold or shared.
